Reading challenge tracker

Your year in books, one spine at a time.

A reading challenge works when it's built from days, not from a big number in January. Choose what you can read each day, and BookWorm shows the books it adds up to.

How the challenge works

One daily habit underneath, one shelf on top.

Start from a daily goal

Pick pages or minutes a day. BookWorm turns it into a number of books for the year, so the target matches a day you can actually keep.

A shelf that fills

Every book you finish this year stands on your shelf as a spine, next to empty places for the ones still to come.

Always know your pace

A gold flag marks where today's pace says you should be. Ahead of it, behind it, or right on it, you see it at a glance.

Every format counts

Paper books, ebooks and audiobooks all count toward the same challenge. Listening on a walk is reading.

Questions about reading challenges

How many books should I aim for this year?

Start from the time you have. Fifteen minutes a day is about a book a month for most readers. If last year was six books, twelve is a real stretch. The challenge ideas guide has more ways to choose.

What if I fall behind?

Nothing breaks. The flag shows where your pace is, and the goal is yours to change. Plenty of readers catch up in summer or on holiday.

Do audiobooks count toward a reading challenge?

In BookWorm they do. Minutes of listening count toward the same daily goal as pages, and a finished audiobook takes its place on the shelf.

Can I share my progress?

You can share each finished book as a library checkout card, and on the first of each month BookWorm shows a recap of the month you read.
