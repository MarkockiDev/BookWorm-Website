Audiobook tracker

Listening is reading. Track it like it.

BookWorm keeps your audiobooks on the same shelf as your paper books, tracked in hours and minutes, counted toward the same daily goal and the same streak.

Made for books you hear

Most reading trackers only understand pages. BookWorm understands where you are in a listen.

Hours and minutes, not pages

Log 3 h 30 m of 11 h 35 m and the progress bar, the calendar and your stats all speak in time.

One goal for every format

Set your daily goal in minutes or pages. Listening and reading both count toward it, so a commute with an audiobook keeps the streak alive.

A timer for each listen

Start a session when you press play. Pause and resume it from the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island, then log where you got to.

Quiet next to your audio

BookWorm's sounds never pause or quieten your audiobook, and the Silent switch silences them completely.

Add a book in seconds

Search by title or author and the cover fills itself in. Choose audiobook, and BookWorm suggests a length from the book's page count, which you can change to match your edition.

Switch a book between paper, ebook and audio whenever you like. Each day keeps the format it was read in, so your history stays accurate.

Questions about tracking audiobooks

Does listening to an audiobook count toward my reading goal?

Yes. BookWorm counts minutes listened and pages read toward the same daily goal, so every format keeps your streak going.

Can I track an audiobook and a paper book at the same time?

Yes. Every book you're reading sits on Home, whatever its format, and you update each one on its own.

How does BookWorm know how long an audiobook is?

When you add one from search, it estimates the length from the book's page count. You can type the exact length from your audiobook app instead.

Does BookWorm play or download audiobooks?

No. Keep listening in the app you already use. BookWorm is where you keep track of it.
