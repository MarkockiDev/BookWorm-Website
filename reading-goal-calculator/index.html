How the calculator works

For a yearly goal it multiplies your books by their average length and spreads those pages over the days left until 31 December, today included. Late in the year it offers next year instead, so the number stays one you can live with. To finish one book, it spreads the pages you have left over the days until your date.

Like the app, it rounds up to the next five, because a goal you can meet beats one you keep missing by a page. Minutes assume about a minute and a half per page of a paperback.

Questions about reading goals

How many pages a day to read 52 books a year?

With books of about 300 pages, 52 books is 15,600 pages, or 43 pages a day across a full year. At a minute and a half per page, that is a little over an hour a day.

How long does it take to read a 300-page book?

Most readers need between five and ten hours, depending on the book and how fast they read. At 30 pages a day it takes ten days.

Do audiobooks count toward my goal?

They do in BookWorm. Listening is tracked in hours and minutes, and your daily goal counts reading and listening together.

What if I fall behind?

Run the calculator again from today. The new number already includes the days you missed, and it is usually smaller than it feels.
