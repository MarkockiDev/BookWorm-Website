The basics

SECTION 01Who we are

BookWorm ("the app", "we", "us", or "our") is a reading tracker for iOS that helps you log books, follow your reading progress, build reading streaks, and organise your personal library. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

This policy applies to the BookWorm iOS application distributed through the Apple App Store. By using BookWorm, you agree to the practices described here.

SECTION 02Our privacy principle

BookWorm is built to be private by design. The app works without an account, without a login, and without sending your library to our servers. Your reading data belongs to you — it stays on your device and, if you choose, syncs privately through your own iCloud account.

Your data

SECTION 03Information stored on your device

Everything you create in BookWorm is stored locally on your device. We do not receive or have access to this content. It includes:

  • Your library: book titles, authors, cover images, formats, genres, and reading status.
  • Reading activity: progress, page and time positions, start and finish dates, reading log, and streaks.
  • Personal touches: a display name and profile photo you optionally add, reading goals, favourites, and app settings such as language, theme, and notification preferences.

Device features, used only when you choose

Camera

If you scan a book barcode, BookWorm uses your camera to read the ISBN. Scanning happens on your device — no photo or video is stored or uploaded.

Photos

When you save a share card, BookWorm asks for add-only photo permission so it can save an image. The app never requests read access to your photo library.

Share cards

If you share a finished book, BookWorm creates the image on your device from that book's title, author, cover, and reading dates — then sends it only where you choose to share it.

Widgets

If you add a BookWorm widget, the app shares only what the widget needs — current book, progress, streak — through a private on-device App Group container. Lock Screen widgets may be visible to anyone who can see your screen.

This information never leaves your device except through the private iCloud sync described below.

SECTION 04iCloud sync (CloudKit)

If iCloud is enabled on your device, BookWorm can sync your library across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, protected by your Apple ID.

  • The developer of BookWorm cannot see, access, or read the contents of your private iCloud database.
  • This sync is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms.
  • You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in your device's iCloud settings.

SECTION 05Analytics

To understand how features are used and to improve the app, BookWorm collects a small amount of pseudonymous usage data using PostHog. Data is processed on PostHog's EU-based cloud for GDPR compliance.

This analytics data:

  • Is not used to track you across other apps or websites — no IDFA, no cross-app tracking.
  • Contains no personal content. We never send book titles, author names, your display name, notes, your profile photo, or the text you type into search. For search, we record only query length and result count.
  • Consists of product-interaction events, such as which screens are opened, which features are used, and onboarding choices, to help us fix problems and prioritise improvements.
  • Includes crash reports. If the app crashes, a technical report (where in the code it stopped, the app and iOS version, and the device model) is sent the next time you open BookWorm, so we can find and fix the problem. It contains none of your library, notes or other personal content.
  • Is associated with a randomly generated app identifier and a small usage profile — for example reading goals, app language and theme, notification settings, library size, books finished, audiobook usage, and whether you have set a profile photo (never the photo itself).

This data is not used for tracking and is never sold or shared for advertising.

Analytics and crash reporting are part of how BookWorm works and there is no setting in the app to turn them off. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping a free app stable and improving it, and we keep the data limited to what is described above. You can object at any time, or ask us to erase the analytics profile for your device, by contacting us using the details below.

Who else is involved

BookWorm relies on a few external services strictly to make features work. We share only what is technically necessary, and we do not sell data to anyone.

Open Library

Enriching a selected book with its page count.

SharedA book's ISBN, or its title and author.

Apple iCloud / CloudKit

Apple App Store & StoreKit

Processing optional one-time tips.

SharedPurchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

RevenueCat

Processing and validating optional tips, and remembering that you tipped.

SharedAn app-generated anonymous user ID and Apple's purchase receipt. Never your payment details.

PostHog (EU)

Pseudonymous usage analytics.

SharedProduct-interaction events and app-level usage properties tied to a random app identifier.

Canny

The optional feature-suggestion board opened from Settings.

SharedOnly what you choose to type there, under Canny's own privacy policy.

These services process data under their own privacy policies. Book searches are sent to Apple and Open Library only when you actively search for or scan a book.

SECTION 07Purchases & tips

BookWorm is free. It offers optional one-time tips through Apple's in-app purchase system. These are single payments, not subscriptions — nothing recurs and nothing is unlocked. All payments are processed by Apple. We never receive or store your credit-card number, billing address, or Apple ID password. Receipts are handled through your Apple account settings.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and remember whether you have tipped, so the app can thank you. RevenueCat receives an app-generated anonymous identifier and Apple's purchase receipt, but never your name, email, or payment details. RevenueCat does not handle payment processing; Apple does.

SECTION 08Notifications

If you enable reading reminders, BookWorm schedules local notifications on your device — for example a morning reading prompt, an evening progress reminder, and a nudge when a reading streak is at risk. These notifications are generated on-device and are not sent through any external server. You can disable them at any time in the app's settings or in your device's system settings.

Reminders also include an occasional weekly note, currently on Sundays, mentioning that BookWorm is free and that you can leave an optional tip if you would like to. It contains no advertising and links only to BookWorm's own tip screen. It is part of the same reminder setting, so turning reminders off turns it off too.

Your control

SECTION 09What we do not do

  • Require an account or email address to use the app.
  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Show third-party advertising.
  • Collect your precise location, contacts, or health data.

SECTION 10Data retention & deletion

Because your library is stored locally and in your own iCloud account, you are always in control:

  • Deleting a book, or all your data, within the app removes it from your device — and syncs that deletion to your iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from that device.
  • To remove synced data everywhere, delete it in the app while signed in to iCloud, or manage BookWorm's data in your device's iCloud settings.
  • Analytics events are tied to a randomly generated app identifier and retained by PostHog on our behalf. If you reset all app data, a new identifier is created and past analytics can no longer be associated with your device.

SECTION 11Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

Because BookWorm does not hold your personal library on our own servers, most of these rights are exercised directly within the app by editing or deleting your data. Resetting all data in the app also resets your analytics identifier. If you would like us to erase the analytics profile associated with your device, or you have any other privacy question, contact us using the details below and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

The fine print

SECTION 12Children's privacy

BookWorm is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to address it.

SECTION 13Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Data in transit to the services listed above is protected using encryption (HTTPS/TLS). iCloud data is secured by Apple's infrastructure and your Apple ID. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

SECTION 14International data transfers

The third-party services we use may process data on servers located in various countries. We have chosen PostHog's EU cloud for analytics to support GDPR compliance. RevenueCat, which validates purchases, processes data in the United States. Where data is transferred internationally, it is done under the safeguards provided by the respective service providers.

SECTION 15Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or in legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. This version was last updated on 26 October 2026. Significant changes may also be communicated within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
