Reading habit tracker

A little every day adds up to a lot.

Pick a daily goal you can keep. BookWorm counts the days you reach it, lights up your calendar, and nudges you once a day, gently.

How the habit takes hold

Every piece is there to make tomorrow's reading a little more likely than today's.

A goal sized for real life

Choose pages or minutes a day, from 5 upwards. BookWorm also turns it into the number of books it adds up to this year.

The streak worm

Each day you reach your goal, the worm grows a segment. Its head lights up the moment today counts.

A calendar of reading days

Every day you read gets a mark, and every book you finish gets a gold ring. A month at a glance shows the rhythm you've built.

One calm reminder

One reminder at lunchtime, written for where you are, and a last call in the evening only when a streak is on the line. Nothing at all once today's goal is met.

Miss a day? Start again tomorrow.

Streaks are a nudge, not a judge. If a day slips, the streak starts again and the next day is a fresh start, with a reminder that says exactly that.

Your best streak stays on your profile, and on the first of each month BookWorm shows a recap of the month you just read.

Questions about reading streaks

What counts as a day in my streak?

Any day you reach your daily goal, in pages or minutes. Reading and listening both count.

What daily reading goal should I choose?

One you could meet on a busy day. Ten to twenty pages, or fifteen minutes, is a good start, and you can raise it any time.

How many reminders will I get?

At most one at lunchtime and, only when your streak is at risk, one in the evening. None on a day you've already reached your goal.

Can I see my streak without opening the app?

Yes. The Home Screen widgets show today's goal as a ring and your current streak.
