Reading log

A reading log you'll actually keep.

Print the paper log for your desk, or let BookWorm keep it for you on your iPhone. Better still, both.

Download PDF (A4)Download PDF (US Letter)

Three pages: books read, a year of reading days, and notes on a book. Print as many as you like.

What's in the printable log

Made for a pencil and a few seconds a day.

Books I've read

Title, author, format, the dates you started and finished, and a rating, with room for twenty books on a page.

A year of reading days

Twelve months, one box a day. Colour in each day you read and watch the year fill up.

Notes on a book

A lined page for one book: a favourite line, what stayed with you, and who you'd give it to.

The log that fills itself in

Paper logs fail in the same way: one busy week and the gaps start. In BookWorm, logging is the same tap you use to save your page, so every reading day, every page and every finished book is recorded without a separate step.

Paper books, ebooks and audiobooks share one log, counted in pages or hours and minutes. Your calendar, streak and yearly shelf are drawn from it.

Questions about reading logs

What should a reading log include?

At least the title, the author and the date you finished. Most readers also like the start date, the format and a quick rating. A daily log adds the pages or minutes you read each day.

Is a reading log the same as a reading journal?

Not quite. A log records what and when you read. A journal adds what you thought. The printable includes a notes page, and BookWorm keeps notes on every book.

Can I use the printable log for my child?

Yes. The year of reading days works well for children: one square to colour in for every day they read.

Does BookWorm count audiobooks in the log?

Yes. Audiobooks are logged in hours and minutes, next to your paper books and ebooks, and they count toward the same daily goal.
